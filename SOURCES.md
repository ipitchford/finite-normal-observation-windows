# Sources and bounded comparison

- [Aldroubi–Huang–Petrosyan (2019)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.10103): Theorem 5.5, Conjecture 5.6 and proof. Invertible self-adjoint antecedent and normal-reductive conjecture; Montel strategy is inherited.
- [Aldroubi–Cabrelli–Krishtal–Molter (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44007-026-00215-y): Theorem 2.13 and Conjecture 2. Survey repeats target; not proof of worldwide priority.
- [Díaz Martín–Medri–Molter (2021)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.01488): Theorems 3.9 and 3.11. Bounded-generator admissibility and self-adjoint time independence; does not directly cover unbounded logarithm.
- [Partington–Pott (2005)](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/ims/bull55/R5502.pdf): Section 5, printed page 30. Exact-observation duration can matter for general semigroups; no refutation claimed.
- [Haak–Ouhabaz (2012)](https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/~bhaak/recherche/HaakOuhabaz.pdf): Introduction and BFC/analytic passages. Additional backward-forward conditioning hypotheses cannot be inferred from this manuscript's frame premise.

Engel–Nagel (2000), Corollary VI.8.12, is used only for approximate controllability, not an exact lower norm bound. The domain review did not independently reopen this source; the manuscript records a relevant-section reading in the research workflow. This distinction is retained rather than claiming all sources were independently read by every checker.
